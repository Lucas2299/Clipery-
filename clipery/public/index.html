FAQ

Questions, answered

How does Clipery decide what makes a good clip?

The AI looks for hook density, energy peaks, pacing and short-form length fit. Each clip gets a viral score from 0–100 so you publish the strongest first.

What’s Rank video?

Rank video takes up to 5 separate clips or links and builds one countdown ranking video — yellow title, left-side numbers, full-length audio, and names on screen.

Can I paste TikTok links?

YouTube links work best for download. TikTok is often blocked by IP — save the file and upload instead.
